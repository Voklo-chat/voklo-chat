Frequently Asked Questions

Is Voklo completely anonymous and free?

Yes. You do not need to share your phone number, email, or social media accounts. Your nickname and Friends list are stored locally in your browser so you stay in control of your privacy.

How does Voklo keep chats safe?

Voklo uses an automatic profanity and spam-link filter to block abusive words and scam URLs before they send. Every chat also includes a 1-tap Report & Disconnect button.

How do Voklo Coins and Vibe Badges work?

Voklo Coins are virtual, non-monetary points earned by having active back-and-forth conversations (reaching 100% on the Chemistry Meter) and receiving positive Vibe Badges when a chat ends.
