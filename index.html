Frequently Asked Questions

Is Voklo completely anonymous and free?

Yes. You never need to share your phone number, email, or social accounts. Your nickname and Friends list stay saved locally in your browser.

How does Voklo keep chats, photos, and calls safe?

Voklo blocks abusive words, phone numbers, and spam links automatically. All photos arrive blurred by default, voice calls require mutual acceptance, and every chat has a 1-tap Report button.

How do Voklo Coins and Vibe Badges work?

Voklo Coins are virtual, non-monetary points earned by reaching 100% on the live Chemistry Meter and receiving positive Vibe Badges from your chat partners.
